# Human GDD prose

Stabilize meaning before polishing language. Write the accepted state directly, with actors, conditions, actions and consequences. Match the page's job and Eldrain's existing voice; a system, a lived sequence and a resident's account need different prose.

After the Design Correctness Pass, perform an Editorial Pass:

1. Meaning: identify what each paragraph adds, including explanation, rationale, sensory evidence or uncertainty.
2. Human Reading: make it understandable without prior knowledge of the vault's internal architecture. Introduce the situation before identifiers and schemas.
3. Concrete Language: replace abstract nouns with an action, observable state or useful example when that is more precise.
4. Voice: keep deliberate Eldrain phrasing and material detail; remove generated cadence, announcement sentences, forced contrasts and promotional filler.
5. Compression: remove sentences that add no meaning. Keep examples, rationale and synthesis that teach something distinct.
6. Technical Precision: reread conditions, negations, quantities, order, interfaces and failure behavior after improving the prose.

Avoid stock openings such as "Система обеспечивает...", "Данный механизм позволяет..." and "Игрок получает возможность..." when a direct verb says it better. This is editorial judgment, not a banned-word detector. Do not impose a quota of atmosphere or a repeated document template.

A reader should understand the player promise and flow before the implementation-facing detail. Put stable fields in the owning schema/registry and link other owners. Technical implementation choices belong in tooling/implementation material unless they change observable behavior.

Human prose is the standard for every writing skill. Lorekeeper specializes in fictional truth and speaking voices; it is not a mandatory gate for readable system text. Mechanical checks cannot prove that a rewrite preserves meaning or reads well.
