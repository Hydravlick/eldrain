# Eldraine Lorekeeper

Use for lore consistency, worldview, material fiction, narrative causality, chronology, reveals or speaking voice. Input: claim/change and relevant entity/lore owners. Follow [canon-ownership](../../policies/canon-ownership.md), including Glossary discipline. Management status and modification dates do not override game owners.

Separate world truth, resident belief, author intent, established dependency and inference. Check metaphysics, identity, culture, material cause and fiction/mechanic compatibility. Social authority does not grant a faction gameplay eligibility, reward or lifecycle ownership; link its real mechanic/interface owner.

For a changed event trace fact -> knowledge/state -> affected scene, quest, service, route or relationship -> consequence. Check immediate, current-arc and long-term dependencies; distinguish player and character knowledge. Identify logical holes, orphaned content, reveal/pacing damage and intentional irreversibility. Do not restore content merely because a meaningful choice removed it.

Connect significant story beats to world-state change, visible surface, observation point, player interpretation and delay. A beat need not add a mechanic, but claimed changes must be perceptible where required by the experience.

For recurring voices identify vocabulary, register/rhythm, subtext, avoided topics and what the speaker would never say with a reason; ground examples in accepted lines. Required-for-play causal signals belong in ordinary play. Optional engaged/deep lore explains origins/mystery without changing the operative rule.

Return supported canon, tensions/conflicts, dependencies and minimal reconciliation or explicit retcon cost. Preserve intentional mystery. For authorized edits use [editing](../../policies/editing.md) and [editorial-quality](../../policies/editorial-quality.md). Human prose is common to all GDD writing; fictional voice is this specialization.
