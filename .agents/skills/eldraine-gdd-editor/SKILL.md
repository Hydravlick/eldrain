# Eldraine GDD Editor

Use for canonical writing, integration or editorial repair after a decision is accepted. Input: decision/revision, owner/source text and protected meaning. Find the home through [canon-ownership](../../policies/canon-ownership.md). Extend its page/registry; a new page needs an independently useful responsibility.

Follow [editing](../../policies/editing.md). Match coverage and voice to the page's job. In design-facing prose, explain concrete play before design abstraction and supporting architecture. Coverage questions guide completeness; do not mechanically look for identical headings across pages. Do not redesign an accepted system while rewriting it.

## Design Correctness Pass

Establish meaning; check ownership, states/logic, operation order, interfaces, terminology and edge cases. Compare source/destination meaning and remove duplication without deleting unique rationale, experience or examples. Link foreign rules instead of copying their resolution. Separate unresolved assumptions from accepted facts. Ask about a necessary product choice only when it is still undecided.

For features read [feature-contract](../../policies/feature-contract.md); for ambiguous responsibility read [design-architecture](../../policies/design-architecture.md). A genuine architecture conflict may need Design Architect; ordinary placement does not.

## Editorial Pass

Apply [editorial-quality](../../policies/editorial-quality.md): Meaning -> Human Reading -> Concrete Language -> Voice -> Compression -> Technical Precision. Check:

- Concrete play before design abstraction: can the reader follow the situation, visible information, action, consequence and next choice?
- Page-specific voice: does this Vision, Feature, System, Mechanic, Registry, Lore or PM page speak in the form its reader needs?
- Technical detail at the appropriate layer: Features explain lived play first; Systems retain precise authoritative resolution; Registries stay focused on data/schema.
- Repeated architecture boilerplate: remove generic ownership, status, TODO and validation explanations that belong in harness guidance.
- Forced contrasts and slogan-like prose: state the design information directly; keep a contrast only when it explains a real distinction.
- Unexplained abstract nouns: connect them to observable actions, conditions and consequences.
- Unnecessary “player should feel” claims: explain what the player knows, risks, sees and can do before naming a useful emotional target.

Preserve intentional voice and useful material, sensory and explanatory detail supported by owners/lore. Do not invent canon to make an example vivid. Reread rules after polishing for changed negation, condition or causality. These checks require reading and judgment, not phrase matching or a uniform page template.

Use [obsidian-workflow](../../policies/obsidian-workflow.md) for properties, links, moves/views and [validation](../../policies/validation.md) for checks. Return actual edits, remaining decisions and verification. An explicitly requested draft/review remains that artifact without inferred edits.
