**The vision is structurally compatible with Eldrain, but only if predictable production remains bounded by meaningful choices that wealth cannot erase.** Material origin, complete finds and city specialists can reinforce one another. They will not do so merely because recipes require extracted ingredients, services have different names, or the resource graph is sparse.

The strongest part is **preparation through concrete transformations**. The weakest part is the implied expectation that a finite material ecosystem can keep generating equally consequential collection decisions indefinitely.

Canon was not edited. Below, **KNOWN** identifies documented rules or scoped observations; **STRONGLY INFERRED** identifies consequences supported by explicit mechanisms; **PLAUSIBLE** identifies credible but untested effects; **SPECULATIVE** identifies possibilities requiring substantial validation.

**1. The strongest surviving vision is an economy of preparation, replacement and discovery.**

An Anomaly presents physically understandable sources and changing opportunities. Players extract both usable objects and materials. The city makes selected outcomes dependable: restoring a working item, assembling a known solution, adapting equipment to a particular problem, or accepting responsibility for unusual cargo. Complete finds retain properties and possibilities that ordinary production cannot freely reproduce.

Cross-Anomaly relationships should usually change **how players undertake a worthwhile expedition**, rather than determine whether they can undertake it at all.

This already fits much of [Economy_Core](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Economy_Core.md), [Loot_Distribution](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Loot_Distribution.md) and the [Core Loop](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/01_Core_Vision/02_Core_Loop.md). Compatibility at that level is **KNOWN**; its ability to sustain interesting play is **PLAUSIBLE**.

Four stronger propositions should be rejected:

- Every material should remain worth collecting by every established player.
- Every Anomaly should remain a necessary resource destination.
- Every valuable complete find should benefit from additional crafting.
- More connections between materials, services and locations necessarily produce more depth.

Some needs should become routine. Some objects should be left behind. The enduring question is whether the player still makes consequential choices about **what to undertake, what to carry and what preparation to commit**.

**2. Acquisition channels need different responsibilities, with deliberate overlap.**

These are recommended boundaries, not new canon.

|Channel or layer|Responsibility that should survive|Boundary that protects the other channels|
|---|---|---|
|**Found complete gear**|Immediate capability when compatible; concrete instance variance; unusual Patterns; usable imperfections; provenance; replacement savings; discoveries that redirect a raid|Its ordinary use must sometimes be the best destination. Being valuable only as a crafting base does not satisfy this responsibility|
|**Materials**|Targetable preparation, repair, sustain, bounded specialization and a small number of understandable future options|Material families cannot become universally interchangeable purchasing power; every material need not support every role|
|**City transformations**|A known useful outcome, restoration or explicit exchange between capabilities|No general route from arbitrary equipment to an exact ideal instance|
|**Blueprints**|Permission to repeat a specific exceptional transformation|They must neither replace physical inputs nor become obligatory infrastructure for ordinary survival|
|**Rarity and ordinary Affixes**|Variation within an existing role|They cannot quietly accumulate into an unrestricted final-power ladder|
|**Modules**|Deliberately chosen physical functions within fit, topology and service constraints|Acquisition does not manufacture installation capacity or erase other modules’ costs|
|**Legendary outcomes**|A separately authored, narrow specialization-changing rule|Acquisition belongs to the particular outcome: named production, event or found carrier can each be appropriate|
|**Relics**|An anomalous object with its own procedure, origin, failure and counterplay|No blanket deterministic Relic catalogue; no assumption that every Relic is portable equipment|

The proposed separation of identity, tuning and exceptional rules is healthy **if maintained at the level of the finished configuration**—**STRONGLY INFERRED**.

The terminology matters. [Gear_Progression](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/07_Gear_Inventory/Gear_Progression.md) and [Weapon_Core (line 64)](/C:/Hobby/Eldrain/05_Combat_Survival/Weapon_Core.md:64) explicitly reserve player-facing T1/T2/T3 for Anomaly phases. Weapon construction classes are **Scrap / Guild / Expedition**; `load_tier` is technical data. `Alter` is an authored variation, and Relic is not another construction class.

The weapon records demonstrate the distinction. The quarantine marker has `load_tier: 1` and an `uncommon..rare` band; the storm-measuring rig has `load_tier: 3`, `rare..epic`, foreign origin and a `stitched_trace` source. These are separate fields with specific relationships, not one interchangeable Tier. See the [pulse-tool instances](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/05_Combat_Survival/Weapons/Pulse_Tool_1H.md) and [condenser instances](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/05_Combat_Survival/Weapons/Condenser_Rig_2H.md).

**3. Material identity should come from layered eligibility, not four competing loot tables.**

|Layer|Contribution|Boundary|
|---|---|---|
|**Biome**|Recurring physical basis: what materials and environmental processes are broadly plausible|Does not decide every manufactured object or monopolise resource identity|
|**POI and container**|Functional origin: what people made, maintained, stored, treated or protected there|Does not automatically increase Rarity because access is difficult|
|**Phase**|Available rooms, procedures, pressure, source maturity and consequences of the catastrophe|Does not upgrade every container or erase the ordinary material base|
|**Mutation line**|Anomalous samples, transformed sources, organisms, procedures and environmental expressions|Does not automatically turn each creature or stage into a new ingredient|

There is also an existing **source-world layer**: civilisation, technology and the dead world’s causal rules. Folding that entirely into biome would lose meaning already present in [Loot_Distribution](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Loot_Distribution.md).

The current ownership direction is sound: biome and POI declare context and compatible profiles; mutation records declare stages and expressions; item records establish actual objects; loot distribution resolves eligibility and placement. Recipes belong to their own registry. Stable services read their permitted capabilities rather than copy the raid’s loot table. **KNOWN.** See [biomes](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/08_World_Generation/Registries/Registry_Biomes.md), [POI metadata](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/08_World_Generation/Registries/Registry_POI_Metadata.md) and [Dual_State_POIs](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/08_World_Generation/Generation/Dual_State_POIs.md).

Two consequences follow:

- **Do not require a bespoke resource for every combination.** Biome × POI × phase × mutation would multiply content and recognition burden without necessarily adding decisions.
- **Separate ecological knowledge from manufacturing output.** Recognising White Response and exploiting its finite response can itself be valuable; its existence does not require a harvestable “White Response crafting material”.

The Port already offers filters, noisy fire and dry detours as different responses to Sowing. That is stronger evidence for the vision’s compatibility than an invented recipe would be. The [mutation owner](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/08_World_Generation/Anomaly/Anomaly_Mutation_Lines.md) also explicitly requires soft countermeasures, retreat opportunities and finite triggered resources.

**4. City addresses remain distinct only when their outputs or responsibilities remain distinct after optimisation.**

|Function|Meaningful transformation|How it collapses|
|---|---|---|
|**Central supplier**|Scarcity or loss → a viable next expedition|Becomes either a frustrating survival gate or the cheapest source of advanced production inputs|
|**Repair**|A particular damaged object → that object restored|Becomes compulsory polishing of every find, or an indirect Affix upgrade|
|**Dismantling**|An unwanted object → an explicitly preserved component or material|Broadly superior salvage makes every found weapon a resource bundle|
|**Medical service**|Injury or contamination → restored readiness; dangerous biological cargo → appropriate care or handling|Ordinary recovery becomes dependent on specialist loot or reputation|
|**Item stabilisation**|Extracted Volatile object → dependable material use through its declared procedure|Becomes a universal tax on all loot, or is confused with healing and legal ownership|
|**Stable/external master**|Suitable material and equipment → a narrow adaptation, assembly or treatment|Offers the same result as another address with a better exchange rate|
|**Broker/grey service**|Disputed custody or unusual responsibility → an explicitly different usable path|Launders everything into equivalent clean goods for a calculable surcharge|
|**Hearth/faction address**|Material, evidence or work → a specified obligation, commissioned outcome, access or trust|Becomes a reputation vending machine whose best rewards can be stockpiled in advance|
|**Blueprint/commissioned production**|Extracted inputs plus a particular permission → an authored result|Becomes a universal endgame catalogue|

This division substantially matches [Vendor_Logic](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Vendor_Logic.md), [Resource_Cycle](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Resource_Cycle.md) and [Pledge_Contracts](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/03_Factions_Societies/Pledge_Contracts.md). The proposed long-term differentiation is **PLAUSIBLE**, not established by those declarations.

An optimiser can assign a personal value to almost anything. The objective cannot be to prevent valuation. It is to prevent a **single stable ordering of addresses** from serving every objective.

Repair versus preserving a component can remain different. Two routes to an identical repaired item usually reduce to price, time and access. That ordinary comparison is acceptable; presenting it as a major strategic decision is not.

Temporary availability is also not differentiation by itself. A master available for an entire Stable cycle can still cause waiting and stockpiling if only that master produces an effectively mandatory result. Protecting the central minimum does not protect access to a particular specialisation.

**Service-based transformation is a promising way to limit production burden—PLAUSIBLE.** It can reuse an existing object and express one legible change. But it only reduces complexity when transformations share bounded interfaces. Authoring compatibility for every Pattern, material, Affix and topology combination simply moves the recipe explosion into service rules.

**5. A sparse capability graph is healthier than a recursive production ladder, but sparsity is insufficient.**

The useful relationship is:

> A source gives material for a specific transformation, which changes a meaningful decision in another expedition.

The danger is an edge that appears optional in the menu but is functionally compulsory in play.

A healthy edge needs all of the following:

- B contains worthwhile play without that exact imported preparation.
- The imported capability changes a route, tactic, exposure or task—not merely an eligibility flag.
- Its benefit retains a relevant cost or incompatibility inside B.
- The alternative is credible for the intended cohort, not merely achievable by exceptional players.
- The source and service are available predictably enough that planning does not become waiting.
- A group cannot erase the intended cost by equipping one carrier while everybody receives an unrestricted benefit.

These conditions are **STRONGLY INFERRED** from substitution, opportunity cost and Eldrain’s existing physical preparation rules.

A modest reduction in failure can become effectively mandatory where failure loses both cargo and a valued Pawn. “Not required to enter” therefore provides weak protection against soft-gating. Examine whether players willingly omit the preparation after understanding its benefits.

**The principle that important edges should terminate in capabilities survives, with exceptions.** An intermediate is justified when it creates its own decision: preserving a fragile component, choosing between uses, changing transport requirements, or completing an interesting local procedure. An intermediate that exists only because the next recipe demands it adds bookkeeping.

There is no defensible universal depth limit. A four-step authored restoration can be more intelligible than a one-step recipe requiring twelve unrelated ingredients.

More importantly, individually shallow recipes can still form a mandatory ladder:

> A makes B’s necessary protection; B makes C’s necessary tool; C supplies the next necessary protection.

The whole progression graph must be inspected for bottlenecks, not just recipe depth.

Do not make every Anomaly depend on another. Independent destinations and overlapping functional alternatives are valuable counterforces against prerequisite farming.

**6. Found and produced gear can coexist throughout the lifecycle, but neither “random versus deterministic” nor “cheap versus expensive” is enough.**

The healthy asymmetry is:

- Production guarantees a **satisfactory declared solution**.
- Finds provide **concrete alternatives whose useful properties are not freely reconstructible**.

A crafted item need not be universally weaker. It can be the most dependable choice for one job. A found item need not be universally stronger. It can be an excellent usable configuration, an unusual Pattern, a compatible replacement, or a different solution that changes the planned expedition.

The decisive question is:

> Once the player knows every recipe and has substantial reserves, what useful property of this found object cannot simply be ordered?

If the answer is only its story, rarity colour or avoided fee, complete finds have weak mechanical protection.

Control over individual axes has different consequences:

|Control|Research verdict|
|---|---|
|**Exact Pattern**|Reasonable for selected production outputs; dangerous if the ordinary catalogue covers every desirable found identity|
|**Construction class**|May be fixed by an authored output; should not become a universal upgrading service|
|**Rarity**|A named outcome can declare it; freely increasing any item’s Rarity undermines acquisition boundaries|
|**Affix family and exact result**|A fixed authored property is materially different from freely editing an existing instance|
|**Module configuration**|Deliberate assembly is already part of Eldrain; it must retain physical topology and service constraints|
|**Environmental adaptation**|Valuable when narrow and costly in the expedition; dangerous when accumulated adaptations eliminate exposure|
|**Final performance envelope**|Must remain constrained by combined behaviour, not merely by each modifier passing an isolated check|

Ordinary Affixes do not need `+Damage` to produce vertical dominance. Better cooling, handling, signature and reliability can jointly improve sustained lethality, survival and resource efficiency. Their separation into families prevents duplication; it does not prove the combined result is a sidegrade. **STRONGLY INFERRED.**

The [Affix grammar](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/07_Gear_Inventory/Affix_Grammar.md) currently protects found properties, allows specific Defect repair, and permits authored fixed outcomes. The [one-variant rule](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Craft_Modifiers.md) is a sensible initial constraint. The evidence does **not** justify broader generic Affix control.

However, one slot per transaction is not sufficient protection if several transactions can sequentially perfect different axes. What matters is the **entire reachable transformation sequence**.

At the opposite extreme, materials become indirect gambling tokens when targeted effort cannot secure a useful role and merely buys another chance at it. Randomness in _finding the inputs_ is compatible with planning; randomness in whether the completed preparation solves its advertised problem is much less so.

For existing gear, repair and bounded adaptation should preserve reasons to use the object. A system where every good find must visit several masters before deployment would preserve economic demand while losing the desired complete-find experience.

For exceptional objects:

- A **manufactured Legendary specialisation** can reasonably have a named limited scheme.
- An **event-bound Legendary carrier** should retain that event or carrier dependency.
- A **Relic whose identity depends on a particular anomalous occurrence** should remain tied to that occurrence or an equally specific authored procedure.
- An **embedded Relic** may remain infrastructure, a dangerous procedure or a public consequence.

No evidence supports a universal “all Legendary found” or “all Relics craftable” rule.

**The comparisons support these boundaries, but do not establish an Eldrain outcome.**

- **Path of Exile, Harvest in 3.13–3.14, 2021.** Implementation combined powerful targeted crafts with rarity restrictions. GGG reported that community pooling made rare crafts effectively accessible, encouraged Discord trading, and displaced other crafting methods. That is a documented developer diagnosis, not a controlled causal study. **Transfer: STRONGLY INFERRED** that scarcity alone cannot contain broad deterministic control when supply can be pooled. Confounders include PoE’s trade economy, permanent item retention within a league, extensive modifiers and ARPG power progression. Eldrain’s risky physical transfer reduces pooling convenience but does not remove coordinated supply. [GGG’s Harvest manifesto](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3069670)
    
- **Destiny 2, crafting revisions in 2023–2024.** Bungie’s crafting system permits specific rolls; its revisions extended enhanced traits to selected dropped weapons, and its later stated intention was for crafting to support the weapon chase as catch-up. This establishes the design problem and proposed responses, not their population-wide success. **Transfer: PLAUSIBLE** that granting production both exact selection and exclusive enhancement privileges devalues drops. Confounders are persistent collections, activity lockouts and no extraction loss. Eldrain should preserve competitive usable finds without importing Destiny’s gear-tier response. [2023 crafting changes](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/article/enhancing-weapon-crafting), [2024 rewards intent](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/article/coregame_rewards)
    
- **Last Epoch, the 0.8.4 redesign, 2021.** Directed crafting was bounded by item-specific Forging Potential; better found bases received more capacity. The developers explicitly removed a cleansing option that would bypass the intended value of existing rare and exalted properties. This demonstrates a mechanism for retaining found inputs, not proof that finished finds remain satisfying. **Transfer: STRONGLY INFERRED** that preserving unmanufacturable properties of a find can support crafting. The important non-transfer is making every desirable object a project to finish: that would violate Eldrain’s stronger complete-find objective. Confounders include ARPG drop volume and crafting-centred progression. [Developer explanation](https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/crafting-changes-coming-to-eternal-legends-update-0-8-4/45597/1)

Its discussion contains players using it to clear stashes and guide newcomers: **KNOWN at that observed cohort**, not a prevalence estimate. Embark subsequently described a trader accepting accumulated gear and specific components, including stash expansion and limited Expedition carryover. That establishes an intended response to accumulated inventory, not proof of a saturation cure. **Transfer: STRONGLY INFERRED** that useful ingredients can still become spreadsheet categories; **PLAUSIBLE** that additional sinks merely postpone saturation if their rewards are finite. [Community guide and discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/ArcRaiders/comments/1pxq0yt/arc_raiders_master_cheat_sheet_v31_expedition_2/), [Embark’s trader announcement](https://arcraiders.com/news/meet-the-new-trader)
    
- **Valheim, biome progression.** The official description deliberately connects biome difficulty, unique resources and equipment crafting. That supports material geography as progression structure. It does not prove that the structure suits repeatable extraction. **Transfer: PLAUSIBLE** for understandable origin and preparation; weak for mandatory sequencing, because progression through a survival world is part of Valheim’s promise. **7. Accumulated capital changes the decisions, not just their frequency.**

The following are **PLAUSIBLE cohort predictions**, suitable for testing rather than assuming:

|Cohort|Likely pickup, storage and transformation behaviour after learning|
|---|---|
|**New player**|Keeps recognisable usable gear and immediate sustain; targets one visible preparation need; hoards unfamiliar material when future usefulness is unclear|
|**Experienced solo**|Maintains a compact reserve, values flexible replacements, targets a small number of useful specialisations, and avoids materials requiring several uncertain services|
|**Coordinated group**|Pools materials, distributes gathering and preparation roles, carries shared tools, and assigns valuable finds to compatible users|
|**Optimizer**|Establishes minimum reserves and conversion rules; targets the best expected return for current goals; ignores supposed alternatives that never change outcomes|
|**High-skill, low-capital player**|Uses cheap lethal gear and execution to replace expensive preparation; prioritises immediately deployable trophies and fast recovery|
|**Wealthy veteran**|Collects bottlenecks, exceptional instances and new capabilities; leaves ordinary materials once reserves cover expected use|
|**Player recovering from repeated losses**|Prefers complete working equipment and immediate sustain; delayed recipes are less useful even when nominally efficient|
|**Late newcomer to a solved economy**|Inherits keep lists and routes before understanding their causes; risks experiencing preparation as homework prescribed by experienced players|

Capital must be separated into **materials, ready replacement kits, blueprints, access and knowledge**. Restricting one does not necessarily constrain the others. A veteran can convert material reserves into finished kits before a service disappears; a group can distribute reserves across accounts.

For a durable material family, the basic relationship is:

> Reserve change = successful inflow − actual consumption − unrecovered loss.

If skilled players consistently add more than they consume or lose, their reserves grow until storage or demand limits intervene. Increasing recipe costs changes that relationship only if it produces a sustainable operating range. A one-time unlock merely creates a temporary demand spike.

Several Eldrain facts make this particularly consequential:

- The stash is shared across Pawns.
- Seasonal resets are outside the active MVP.
- Equipment has no general timer-maintenance obligation.
- Ordinary extracted drained batteries recharge free in the Hub; firing them is therefore not automatically a recurring material sink.
- Pawn death does not consume the whole account stash.

These are **KNOWN** from [Stash_Architecture](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/07_Gear_Inventory/Stash_Architecture.md), [Sinks_Insurance](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Sinks_Insurance.md) and [Magic_Batteries (line 182)](/C:/Hobby/Eldrain/05_Combat_Survival/Magic_Batteries.md:182).

Also, equipment taken from a dead player and extracted by another is a **transfer**, not destruction of population-wide capital.

Consequently:

- **Architectural saturation:** all worthwhile configurations are finitely obtainable, durable and fully reproducible; stockpiling removes every material dependency relevant to the veteran’s chosen play.
- **Tunable saturation:** recurring consumption and genuine losses already sustain demand, but current quantities make replenishment too easy or too onerous.
- **Healthy sufficiency:** a veteran stops worrying about ordinary fasteners but still chooses between incompatible preparations and worthwhile expedition objectives.

The proposal should explicitly permit healthy sufficiency. Requiring every experienced player to remain materially insecure conflicts with continuity and risks turning success into a larger maintenance bill.

Knowledge should also be allowed to succeed. A wiki can solve recipes. It should not solve every expedition’s decision, because current routes, local evidence, cargo, opposition and committed equipment still matter.

**8. The major failure modes emerge through adaptation.**

|Failure|First-order effect|Second-order adaptation|Primary diagnosis|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Production dominance**|A known recipe offers the preferred configuration|Players scrap complete gear, pool bottlenecks and prebuild replacements; the raid becomes procurement|**Architectural** when control removes all meaningful differences; **tuning** when only particular recipes dominate|
|**Loot lottery dominance**|Rare complete items greatly outperform reliable alternatives|Players chase jackpot sources and disregard preparation materials except as fees|**Architecture or tuning**, depending on whether essential capabilities are exclusive to jackpots|
|**Everything is an ingredient**|Every object advertises multiple future uses|Players hoard, consult lists and remember ingredient tags instead of physical meaning|**Content-production and UX**; architectural if universal ingredient participation is mandatory|
|**Everything is a price**|Services produce mutually substitutable outcomes|Community tools rank conversion chains and cargo efficiency; one route feeds the rest|**Architectural** if outcomes are fungible; UX cannot repair it|
|**Preparation soft-gating**|Imported equipment sharply reduces failure in B|Groups require it; solo players postpone B; newcomers follow compulsory preparation guides|**Architectural** when alternatives are not credible; tuning only where genuine alternatives already exist|
|**Capital saturation**|Stock covers foreseeable needs|Veterans skip material sources, batch service use and disregard much of Anomaly origin|**Architectural** for finite durable completion; tuning for recurring flows|
|**Solved farming route**|One route supplies several high-demand inputs efficiently|Guides concentrate farmers and predators; weaker players inherit the danger while organised groups control throughput|**Content distribution, tuning and player ecology**; architecture if one source is indispensable|
|**Service availability trap**|Inputs are owned but the required master is absent|Players retain speculative cargo or batch-produce when the master returns|**Architectural availability and UX**, not merely service duration|
|**Transformation combinatorics**|Each adaptation seems individually modest|Content needs compatibility exceptions, stacking rules, new previews and regression coverage|**Content-production**, becoming architectural if arbitrary composition is promised|

Most of these causal chains are **STRONGLY INFERRED conditionally**; their prevalence in Eldrain remains untested.

The “no junk” hypothesis survives only in its narrower form. Avoiding a dominant category whose sole purpose is generic liquidation can improve the relationship between origin and use. But **a recipe is not sufficient evidence of meaningful value**.

A useful boundary is:

- A standard object can have one obvious use or destination.
- Selected active barter materials can support a small number of real alternatives.
- Unusual or disputed objects can justify deeper investigation.
- Ordinary sale remains a legitimate liquidity fallback.
- Some loot can be easy to dismiss without hidden future punishment.

The current two-to-three-purpose requirement should not become a content quota. Artificial second uses increase knowledge burden without improving decisions. Nor should UI hide recipes to preserve mystery: that makes the wiki more authoritative than the game.

**9. Current canon supports the direction, but contains boundaries and unresolved coverage that the proposal cannot silently cross.**

- **Permanent blueprint knowledge is not the current model.** [Blueprints](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/06_Economy_Loot/Blueprints.md) specifies physical limited-use instructions; permanent account unlocks and copying knowledge are deferred. The proposal’s repeatability is compatible only if this distinction is preserved or explicitly reconsidered.
    
- **Generic modification of existing items would expand the current contract.** Specific repair and fixed variants exist. General control over Affix families, Rarity or repeated improvement does not. The research supports investigating bounded services, not treating unrestricted retrofit as already authorised.
    
- **Found Thermos gear is not automatically immediately usable.** A loose module remains cargo until Hub assembly; a captured assembly requires fit and service validation. The complete-find promise must accommodate this physical exception. [Thermos_System](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/07_Gear_Inventory/Thermos_System.md), [Thermos_Assembly](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/07_Gear_Inventory/Thermos_Assembly.md)
    
- **Optional cross-Anomaly preparation does not mean optional physical protection.** Gate Check can produce lethal outcomes without the required environmental preparation. The research recommendation is to avoid dependence on one imported answer, not remove environmental requirements. [Gate_Check](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/08_World_Generation/Generation/Gate_Check.md)
    
- **Continuity is more specific than a cheap shop.** The fixed Welfare loan has an eligibility predicate, cannot be converted into saleable material, and preserves ordinary extraction capability. It is not an advanced kit entitlement. [Spawn_Logic (line 91)](/C:/Hobby/Eldrain/04_Player_Entities/Spawn_Logic.md:91)
    
- **Local price benefits already exist in the reputation direction.** [Reputation_Rules](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/03_Factions_Societies/Reputation_Rules.md) permits cheaper or otherwise improved local services. That does not contradict the ban on a universal best trader, but price differences cannot be counted as proof of distinct transformation roles.
    
- **`SOURCE_CONFLICT` — terminology drift:** [Gear_Progression](C:/Hobby/Eldrain/07_Gear_Inventory/Gear_Progression.md) reserves T1/T2/T3 for Anomaly phases in §3 but still uses “T1 weapon”, “T1 Specialist” and T1/T2/T3 weapon-cycle comparisons later. Therefore the strength of module-based preparation cannot yet be established numerically.

**10. Research cannot decide several product commitments.**

The necessary decisions are:

- **Veteran sufficiency:** Is it acceptable for established players to stop targeting ordinary materials? If not, what recurring burden is acceptable without undermining the desired game?
- **Discovery protection:** Which useful properties or authored objects should ordinary deterministic production never reproduce?
- **Complete-find experience:** How often should a desirable find be deployable without transformation, allowing for legitimate fit and stabilisation requirements?
- **Preparation advantage:** How much greater risk without specialist preparation is acceptable before the alternative becomes nominal?
- **Blueprint meaning:** Is the scarce object knowledge, a consumable physical instruction, permission, or access to a particular procedure?
- **Service absence:** Is postponing a specialisation acceptable, or must another functional route remain available?
- **Cooperative compression:** How much should groups be allowed to reduce individual gathering and preparation demands?
- **Economy horizon:** Is long-term play driven primarily by replenishment, discovery, changing objectives or some accepted combination?

Any future safe player market would require reopening the conclusions. It substantially changes substitution, pooling and the relationship between personal extraction and acquisition.

**11. The smallest useful experiment should attack the model with knowledge and wealth from the start.**

Use a small, disposable decision prototype before building a complete economy:

- Three Anomalies with different sources and route problems.
- A small material set and several complete gear instances.
- Central recovery, two distinct specialist transformations, and one provenance or obligation decision.
- One bounded cross-Anomaly capability, with a credible alternative approach.
- One limited blueprint and a representative fit constraint.
- No recursive intermediate chain in the initial version.

These are experimental fixtures, not proposed canon.

Run the same scenario under three conditions:

1. **Low reserves and initially incomplete knowledge.**
2. **Complete recipe knowledge and coordinated sharing.**
3. **Complete knowledge, substantial material and replacement reserves, followed by losses and a changed Anomaly configuration.**

Compare two acquisition treatments while holding expected resource opportunity broadly comparable:

- A bounded service treatment that preserves instance properties.
- A stronger deterministic treatment that can reproduce more of the desirable finished configuration.

Track actual choices: route, pickup, retention, liquidation, transformation, deployment, preparation omitted, and willingness to change plan after finding complete gear. Ask players to explain what each selected object will let them do.

The model should be rejected or narrowed if:

- Complete gear remains attractive mainly as scrap or a base awaiting mandatory improvement.
- B’s alternative approach is understood but consistently rejected as unreasonable without A’s preparation.
- Full reserves erase origin from choices across materially different scenarios.
- Most address comparisons become conversion arithmetic.
- Players understand the world but still require recipe lists for ordinary pickup decisions.
- Removing an intermediate preserves every meaningful choice.
- Recovery technically permits entry but produces only repeated subsistence farming.

A paper or lightweight interface prototype can falsify the informational and economic assumptions. It cannot establish combat viability: the preparation-versus-alternative question then needs one playable encounter with representative pressure.

The recommendation to carry into that experiment is **bounded predictable transformation, valuable complete instances, and a sparse graph with credible alternatives**. Broader Affix control, universal ingredient usefulness, permanent blueprint progression and deeper recursive chains have not earned a place in later architecture work.
